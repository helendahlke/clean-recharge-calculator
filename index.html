Assumptions: nitrate-free incoming water; the entire selected soil nitrate-N inventory is flushed and uniformly mixed with all applied water; all applied water becomes deep percolation. Soil profile depth is assumed to be 3 feet for interpreting the nitrate inventory; bulk density is not needed when inventory is already reported in lb N/acre. Actual nitrate pulses, soil water retention, preferential flow, biogeochemical reactions, and groundwater mixing are not represented. The 10 mg/L value is the U.S. drinking-water maximum contaminant level for nitrate expressed as N, not a certification of recharge safety. The category labels are illustrative, not agronomic thresholds.
